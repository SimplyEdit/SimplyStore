## Behavior and correctness

- All normal opens/loaders/inspections require a manifest and required hashes;
  previous false toggles cannot disable verification. Conversion creates the
  manifest without --integrity. Location remains configurable.
- Commands append final canonical/index digests as one durable batch; canonical
  digest reuse avoids redundant hashing at the manifest boundary. Recovery defers
  publication to its coordinator, checking retained expected hashes first.
- The explicit stopped-store initializer acquires ownership, checks committed
  history, framing, available indexes, IDs and record contents, syncs the prefix,
  rechecks the inventory and atomically publishes its hashes. It does not execute
  handlers, replay commands, rewrite data/logs/indexes, or replace a manifest.
- Invalid/stale indexes, duplicate IDs, malformed unread bodies, pending history,
  unexplained files, existing ownership, and changing files reject initialization.
  Prepublication validation failure releases ownership; uncertain publication
  retains it. Missing indexes remain reconstructable from verified data.
- The manifest synchronization failure test rejects command success. The batching
  test counts one manifest append/open and one data synchronization for all three
  artifact hashes, alongside the existing parent-directory synchronization.

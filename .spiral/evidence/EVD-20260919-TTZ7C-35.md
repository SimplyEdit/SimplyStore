## Structural and complexity review

`server.mjs` falls from 738 to 317 lines and no longer stores dataset buffers or
metadata. `StoreRuntime` is one new 597-line cohesive class with 26 constructor,
public-story and supporting methods. The central production scope adds 207 net
lines: the server loses 421, the runtime adds 597, and the shared one-shot worker
plus status constant add 31. Focused runtime tests add 261 lines.

The added lines make previously nested assumptions explicit: configuration and
mechanisms, owned state, named lifecycle transitions and a direct verification
seam. No second stateful class, generic container or per-operation module was
introduced. One-shot load/command worker mechanics share `runWorker`; long-lived
query pools remain separate because their lifecycle differs.

The final method review retained supporting names only where they either recur
(`appendCommandStatus`) or let a coordinating method avoid filesystem, JSONTag,
inspection or worker-thread details. The trivial unavailable-result method was
inlined. `acceptCommandInOrder` names the serialized inner story precisely.

The expanded review replaces the 238-line `inspectStore()` body with a two-line
compatibility entry point and a 21-line `StoreInspector.inspect()` story. Its
supporting methods are at most 39 lines. `store-inspection.mjs` grows from 404 to
511 lines because path, history, integrity, reconstruction and reporting state is
now explicit on one cohesive object instead of implicit in a large closure.

The 234-line `applyRecovery()`, 123-line `restoreBackup()` and 124-line
`releaseOfflineLocks()` bodies become small exported entry points.
`RecoveryApplication.run()`, `BackupRestoration.run()` and
`OfflineLockRelease.preview()` state their workflows in 12–20 lines; their
supporting methods are at most 45 lines. `admin-recovery.mjs` grows from 862 to
1123 lines. This adds three internal concepts, each corresponding to a distinct
stateful administrator operation, while avoiding parameter-heavy free helpers,
a generic base class or a service container. The remaining exported recovery
functions stay as functions because their sequential stories are already clear.
